Can I end this lease early if I get a job in another city?
Plainly

Yes, but it will cost you. The lease lets you leave early only if you give 60 days’ written notice and pay a termination fee equal to two months’ rent, which is $3,700.

A new job is not listed as a reason to skip the fee. Only military deployment and unsafe living conditions are.

p. 5 · Section 9.1 p. 5 · Section 9.3
Worth checking: your state may give tenants extra rights to leave early. A local tenant lawyer can confirm.
What does “joint and several liability” mean here?
Plainly

If you share the apartment with roommates who also signed, each of you is responsible for the whole rent, not just your share. If one person stops paying, the landlord can ask any of you for the full amount.

9. Early Termination
9.1 Tenant may terminate this Lease before the end of the Term by giving the Landlord sixty (60) days written notice and paying a fee equal to two (2) months Rent.
9.3 No fee shall apply where the Tenant is called to active military duty.
